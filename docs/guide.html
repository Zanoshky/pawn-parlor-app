01

Install

Get Pawn Parlor on Google Play. The Play title is Board Card Games: Pawn Parlor, and the icon on your phone says Pawn Parlor. It runs on Android 10 or newer, on phones and tablets.

Pawn Parlor is a Trusted Web Activity of games.zanoski.com. In plain words, it opens the Pawn Parlor website in a full screen window that runs on Chrome. The app always opens in Chrome when Chrome is on the phone, even if another browser is your default, because only Chrome can take the payment through Google Play. So keep Chrome installed and up to date. If a game looks wrong, the Runbook has the fix.

The app has no ads. Playing against bots is free. Online play against people needs the Pawn Parlor Online subscription, see online play. The website itself stays free in a browser and does not sell anything.

02

First start and signing in

You can play bots with no account. An account adds online play, a record that follows you between devices, and turn alerts.

  1. Tap Sign In. The panel is called Sign in to your account.
  2. Pick the Login or Register tab. To register you need a Username, an Email and a Password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Tick the box that says you are 13 or older and agree to the Terms, including the chat rules. Then tap Create Account.
  4. Open the verification link we email you. It works once and expires after 24 hours. You need it before you can play online. If it ran out, tap Send a new link.

Short on time? Tap Play as Guest. A guest account needs no email and is deleted three hours after it starts. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password?, enter your email and tap Send Reset Link.

Your account name and your subscription stay together. If you subscribe, sign in with the same account each time.

03

The home screen

The home screen puts what you were doing first.

  • Continue playing lists games in progress, with Your turn when it is up to you. Tap Resume to carry on.
  • Quick Match finds you a game against whoever is free. It is part of online play.
  • Buttons for Live, Leaderboards and The roster, and a link to all the games.
  • My record shows your wins, rate, streak and badges, with links to My games and Leaderboards.
  • The Daily challenge card, with your streak.

On a wide screen the same places sit in a rail on the side: All games, My games, My records, Achievements, Live, Daily challenge, Leaderboards and The roster.

04

Finding a game

Open All games. The library has 66 games, from 1 to 8 players. Classics include Chess, Checkers, Ludo, Nine Men's Morris, Snakes and Ladders, Dots and Boxes, Sea Battle and Tic-Tac-Toe. There are card and dice games such as Durak, Belot and Liar's Dice, and many more trick taking, set collecting and bluffing games. Puzzles include Sudoku, Minesweeper and Nonogram.

  • Type in Search games to find a game by name.
  • How many players narrows by player count: 1 player, 2 players, 3 players, 4 players or 5 or more.
  • How long offers Under 20 min, 20-40 min and 40 min or more.
  • Order sorts by Suggested, New to you, Most played or A to Z.
  • Tap the heart on a game to keep it under Your favourites.
  • Learn in 60s on a card teaches the game in a minute.

A card also says how many people are playing it now, and offers Play now, Play with friends and Set up.

05

Playing a bot

Every game plays against bots, free, with or without a subscription.

  1. Pick a game. On the setup screen choose Play vs Bots. Some games also offer Play Solo or Play Local.
  2. Use Add Player and Choose an opponent to pick who sits at the table. Each bot has a level: Easy, Medium, Hard or Boss.
  3. Some games let you set a Custom Seed (optional) or a Head start for a seat.
  4. Start. The game says who goes first, then plays.

A bot remembers you. The setup screen tells you how many times you have met, and who leads your series. The roster lists every bot, with its record against all players and against you.

When you leave a game, Quit asks Leave Game? and says your progress is saved. Tap Leave and later Resume it from the home screen. Local games need no account and no connection after the first load.

06

During a game

  • Rules and How to Play open the rules for that game. Rules & guide has a search box.
  • Hint suggests a move, if you turned on Suggest a move in Settings, Play. It only shows when you play alone against the computer.
  • Concede opens Resign and Offer a draw. A draw needs everyone to agree. Resigning ends the game and counts as a loss.
  • Game log lists what happened. Scores shows the score.
  • On a board, Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit board to screen help on a small phone.
  • When it ends you see Play again, Return Home and, for finished online games, Replay.
07

The daily challenge

One deal, the same for everyone, until midnight UTC. Open Daily challenge and tap Play today's challenge. It is played against a hard bot.

  • Winning extends your streak. A day you played and lost, or a day you missed, sets the streak back to zero. A day not yet played does not break it until the day rolls over.
  • The page shows Current, Best and Days played, and a strip of the last days.
  • Share result copies a short line you can send to a friend.
  • Signed in, your streak follows you between devices. Signed out, it is counted on this device only.

The daily challenge is free.

08

Live and watching

Open Live to see what is being played right now, and the rooms you can still sit down in. If nothing is going on, the page says Nothing live right now and offers Start a lobby.

Tap a game to watch it. You see the board update as they play, you cannot act in the game, and the players do not see you. Anyone with a watch link can look, no account needed. To share your own game, use Share this game; there is a switch to list it on the home page, off by default. Watching is free.

There is a small Spectator chat just for the people watching. The players cannot see it.

09

Records, leaderboards and replays

  • My records (the page is called Your record) shows games won, points, streak, time played, badges and a By game table.
  • Achievements lists every badge and how close you are. Badges unlock profile icons and titles.
  • Leaderboards shows most wins, by game and by season. Online games only. A game counts as ranked only when at least two real accounts sit at the table.
  • My games lists Recent games and what is waiting on you. Tap Replay to watch a finished online game back. Download CSV saves the list.
  • Replays are kept for a number of days, shown on the page. The result is kept for good.

Reading leaderboards and watching replays is free.

10

Your profile and friends

Your profile page shows games, won, win rate, best streak, Mastery, Achievements, Your rivals and Recent games. Who can see this page lets you pick Public, Friends or Private.

To change your name, Icon, Title or Colour, open Settings, Account, and tap Save profile. Change password is there too.

After an online game, open a player and tap Add friend. Friends sit under Settings, Account, Friends: Pending requests with Accept, and Played with recently. Friends can invite you straight into a lobby with Invite, without a code.

11

Online play with the subscription

Pawn Parlor Online is a monthly subscription, 0.99 a month. Google Play shows the price in your currency before you buy. It unlocks, in the Android app:

  • Quick Match, lobbies and friend invites in every game
  • Chat, rated games and the online leaderboards
  • Spectating and replays you share
  • Bots stay free, with or without it

Subscribe

  1. Open Settings, then Account. The card Pawn Parlor Online says Not subscribed. Bots stay free. Tap Subscribe with Google Play.
  2. Or tap any online action. A sheet called Play people online opens. Tap Subscribe with Google Play, or Not now, play the bots.
  3. Google Play asks you to confirm. When it works the sheet says You are in. The card then says Active with the renewal date.

If Google Play has not taken the money yet, the card says Payment pending. Online play unlocks when Google Play confirms it. After you cancel, the card says Active, ends with the last day.

It renews every month until you cancel. You keep online play to the end of the month you paid for. The purchase is made through Google Play only. The website does not sell anything.

Host a game

Choose a game, then Host. You get a lobby with a code. Share it with Copy invite link, Share invite, Copy code or a QR code (tap Show a bigger QR code). A friend who opens the link joins as a guest with no sign up. List publicly lets strangers find the game, Make invite only limits it to people with the code. Add a bot fills a seat. The host taps Start Game.

Join a game

Tap a link or scan a QR code. Or choose Join, type the code under Lobby code and go. Open games lists public lobbies; if it says No open games right now, host one or start a search. We cannot promise a busy lobby at every hour.

12

Chat and safety

Chat is for people playing together. The rules are on the Terms page: be kind, no hate and no harassment, no private details, nothing sexual. Break them and you can be muted or removed from the games. You must be 13 or older to make an account or play online.

  • Open the options for a message from another player. Report this message sends the last 20 messages of this table to the admin page, and the player is muted for you at once.
  • Block name means a blocked player never sees your chat or lands at your table again.
  • Turn chat off for this game hides chat for that game. Turn chat on brings it back.
  • To undo, open Settings, Account, Blocked players. Tap Unblock or Unmute. Muted players are the ones you reported.
  • If a moderator removed you from chat, the app says You cannot chat right now. and, for a time limit, when you can chat again. You can still play.
  • Chat messages are deleted three hours after they are sent.
  • Guests can read chat but not write in it. The chat box says Create an account to chat, with a Create an account button.
13

Settings

Tap the button named Settings at the top of the screen (on a small screen it is named Menu and settings). Every section is on one page, and Search settings finds a row by name. Sections:

Account
The Pawn Parlor Online card, your profile, Change password and Friends with Blocked players.
Play
Sound with Mute and Unmute, volume faders, Vibration, Suggest a move and Keyboard shortcuts.
Notifications
Turn alerts and Turn reminder emails. Sign in first. We do not promise turn alerts inside the app yet.
Accessibility
High contrast, Colour vision assist and Motion (System, Reduced or Full). Results are shown with an icon and a word, never colour alone.
App
Language (English, German, Croatian, Italian or Russian), Install app, Full screen and Force update.
Privacy and data
Sign out everywhere, Download my data, Delete my account, Privacy and your data and Terms.

Footer button: Log out, or Sign in if you are signed out.

14

Your data and deleting your account

Download your data

  1. Open Settings, then Privacy and data.
  2. Tap Export on Download my data.
  3. You get everything the server holds about this account as a JSON file.

Delete your account

Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

  1. Cancel the subscription: Settings, Account, Manage or cancel in Google Play.
  2. Open Settings, Privacy and data. Tap Delete on Delete my account.
  3. Tap Yes, delete it. Your profile, record, achievements and finished games go with it. It cannot be undone.

Games other people also played are kept, with your name replaced by "[deleted]". The same two actions are on the Privacy page, as Download my data and Delete my account. No account? Nothing is held about you beyond what is in your own browser data. For help, write to support@zanoski.com.

15

Privacy and terms

The privacy page says: no cookies, no advertising, no third party trackers, and nothing sold or shared. Open it from Settings, Privacy and data, Privacy and your data, or at games.zanoski.com/privacy.

  • An account stores your username, your email if you gave one, a hash of your password, your profile colour and icon, and a record of the online games you played.
  • Guest accounts are deleted three hours after they are created. Games played signed out stay on the device.
  • On your device are small settings such as sound, display choices and your play history. Play history is optional and you can withdraw it.
  • The Terms (Terms) cover accounts, age 13 or older, chat rules, what is free, the subscription, and Austrian law: games.zanoski.com/terms.
