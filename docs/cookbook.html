Recipes

Cookbook

Short recipes, a few taps each. Paths like Settings, Account, Blocked players, Unblock are the exact words on screen.

Play a bot

  1. Tap All games and find a game.
  2. Tap the game, then Play vs Bots.

Free, no account.

Pick a bot and its level

  1. On the setup screen tap Add Player.
  2. Tap Choose an opponent and pick one.
  3. Set the level: Easy, Medium, Hard or Boss.

Find a game fast

  1. Open All games.
  2. Type in Search games, or set How many players and How long.
  3. Tap the heart to keep it under Your favourites.

Carry on a saved game

  1. Open the home screen.
  2. Under Continue playing tap Resume.

Your progress is saved when you leave.

Do the daily challenge

  1. Tap Daily challenge.
  2. Tap Play today's challenge.
  3. After the game tap Share result to copy your result.

Watch a live game

  1. Tap Live.
  2. Tap a game that is being played.
  3. You see the board update. You cannot act.

Free. If it says Nothing live right now, try later.

Watch a game back

  1. Tap My games.
  2. Under Recent games tap Replay.

Replays of finished online games are kept for a number of days.

Subscribe to online play

  1. Open Settings, Account.
  2. On the Pawn Parlor Online card tap Subscribe with Google Play.
  3. Confirm in Google Play.

0.99 a month. Bought in the Android app through Google Play only.

Invite a friend by link or QR

  1. Choose a game and tap Host.
  2. Tap Copy invite link or Share invite.
  3. Or tap Show a bigger QR code and let your friend scan it.
  4. When the seat fills, tap Start Game.

A friend who opens the link joins as a guest.

Join with a code

  1. Tap Join.
  2. Type the code under Lobby code.
  3. Wait for Waiting for the host to start the game....

Quick Match

  1. On the home screen tap Quick Match.
  2. Wait for Match found.

If nobody comes, tap Play with bots or Notify me.

Report a chat message

  1. Open the options for the message.
  2. Tap Report this message.

The player is muted for you at once.

Block a player

  1. Open the options for the message.
  2. Tap Block name.

They never see your chat or land at your table again.

Unblock a player

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Account, Blocked players.
  3. Tap Unblock.

Unmute a player

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Account, Blocked players.
  3. Find the name marked Muted and tap Unmute.

Turn chat off for a game

  1. Open the options for a message.
  2. Tap Turn chat off for this game.

Bring it back with Turn chat on.

Add a friend

  1. After an online game open the other player.
  2. Tap Add friend.

They accept under Settings, Account, Pending requests.

Mute the sound

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Play, Sound, tap Mute.

Tap Unmute to bring it back.

Turn hints on

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Play, Suggest a move, turn it on.

Then Hint shows in games you play alone against the computer.

Make it easier to see

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Accessibility, High contrast.

Colour vision assist adds patterns. Motion set to Reduced cuts animation.

Change the language

  1. Open Settings.
  2. App, Language.
  3. Pick English, German, Croatian, Italian or Russian.

Change your name, icon or colour

  1. Open Settings, Account.
  2. Change Username, Icon, Title or Colour.
  3. Tap Save profile.

Change your password

  1. Open Settings, Account.
  2. Under Change password type your Current password and your New password.
  3. Tap Change password.

Hide your profile

  1. Open your profile page.
  2. Under Who can see this page pick Friends or Private.

Download your data

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Privacy and data, Download my data, Export.

Cancel the subscription

  1. Open Settings, Account.
  2. Tap Manage or cancel in Google Play.

Or in Google Play: your picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Pawn Parlor Online, Cancel.

Delete your account

  1. Cancel the subscription first (recipe above).
  2. Open Settings, Privacy and data.
  3. Tap Delete on Delete my account, then Yes, delete it.

It cannot be undone.

Get the newest version of the site

  1. Open Settings.
  2. App, Force update, tap Update.

It clears the cache and reloads the latest version.
