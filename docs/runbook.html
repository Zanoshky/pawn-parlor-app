I subscribed but online play is still locked

The app asks Google Play about your subscription when it starts, and again when you sign in or out. There is no separate restore button. It has to see the same Google account that paid, and the same Pawn Parlor account.

  1. Close Pawn Parlor fully (swipe it away in recent apps) and open it again.
  2. Open Settings, Account. The Pawn Parlor Online card should say Active. If it says Checking your subscription, wait a moment.
  3. Sign in with the Pawn Parlor account you want to use. Signing in attaches the purchase to it.
  4. Check the Google account. In Google Play tap your picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Pawn Parlor Online must be listed under the account that is signed in on this phone.
  5. If Google Play says the payment is pending, it has not gone through yet. Wait for it.
  6. Update Chrome and Google Play from Play, then open the app again.

Still locked? Write to zanoski.marko@gmail.com with your phone model and the date you bought, but never send card details.

Cancel the subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play, not by the app or the website.

  1. Open Settings, Account, and tap Manage or cancel in Google Play.
  2. Or open Google Play, tap your picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Pawn Parlor Online, and Cancel subscription.

You keep online play to the end of the month you paid for. Bots stay free. Deleting your account does not cancel it, so cancel first.

Get a refund

Refunds follow the Google Play refund policy, and Google Play makes the decision.

  1. Within 48 hours of the purchase, ask on Google Play's refund page for the order. Your orders are at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.
  2. After 48 hours, or if the page does not let you, email zanoski.marko@gmail.com with the order number and the date. Do not send card details.
  3. Cancel the subscription too, so it does not renew.

A URL bar shows at the top

The app is a full screen window on Chrome. It only goes full screen when Chrome has checked that the app and the website belong together. If that check fails, or Chrome is out of date, Chrome shows an address bar, and sometimes the short message Digital asset links verification failed.

  1. Open Google Play, search Chrome and tap Update.
  2. Update Pawn Parlor from Google Play too.
  3. Close Pawn Parlor fully and open it again.
  4. Do not disable Chrome. The app needs it.

Still there? Report it with your phone model, Android version and Chrome version.

The page has no styling, or the board is broken

The app runs on Chrome. An old Chrome cannot draw the pages, so you see plain text, no colours or a broken board.

  1. Open Google Play, search Chrome and update it.
  2. Open Settings, App, Force update, and tap Update.
  3. Close the app and open it again.

I am offline

Online games are played on a server, so they pause without a connection. Games against bots run on your phone.

  1. A banner says you are offline and online games are on hold, and the dialog You are offline explains it.
  2. Tap Wait for the connection, or Play offline to play a bot.
  3. Local games are saved on the device and counted in your on-device record.

Nothing is lost: your online table is still there when the connection returns. Local games need no connection after the first load. Open the app once with a connection after you install it.

A game says connecting or reconnecting

A weak connection, or the server being busy.

  1. Check your wifi or mobile data.
  2. Tap Try again if you see This game could not be opened.
  3. Open the Status page: games.zanoski.com/status says whether the platform is working.

My opponent left

Online games give a player a short time to reconnect.

  1. Wait for the timer next to disconnected.
  2. When it ends, tap Claim victory.

The verification link does not work

A link works once and expires after 24 hours.

  1. Tap Send a new link on the verify page, or Send the link again after registering.
  2. Open the newest email only.

I cannot chat

Chat may be off for the game, or a moderator removed you from chat.

  1. Look for Chat is off for this game. and tap Turn chat on.
  2. If it says You cannot chat right now., the message also shows when you can chat again. You can still play.

The lobby list is empty

Nobody listed a game right now. We cannot promise a busy lobby.

  1. Tap Refresh.
  2. Start a search, or host a game and invite a friend by link or QR.
  3. Or play a bot, which is always free.

No turn alerts

Turn alerts are not promised inside the app yet.

  1. Open Android settings, Apps, Pawn Parlor, Notifications, and allow them.
  2. In Settings, Notifications, tap Turn on under Turn alerts.
  3. Or turn on Turn reminder emails.

The app looks out of date

The pages are cached for speed.

  1. Open Settings, App, Force update, Update.

No sound, or too much vibration

Sound or vibration is set low or muted.

  1. Open Settings, Play.
  2. Under Sound tap Unmute and check Overall.
  3. Turn Vibration off if you do not like it.

I blocked someone by mistake

Block hides their chat and keeps them away from your table.

  1. Open Settings, Account, Blocked players.
  2. Tap Unblock.
