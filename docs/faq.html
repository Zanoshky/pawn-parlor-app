Questions

Is Pawn Parlor free?

The app is free to install. Playing against bots is free, and so are the daily challenge, live games, leaderboards and replays. Online play against people needs the Pawn Parlor Online subscription, 0.99 a month. There are no ads.

What does the subscription give me?

Quick Match, lobbies, invites by link or QR, ranked games, chat, friends and the online leaderboards. See online play.

Where do I buy it?

Inside the Android app, through Google Play only. The website does not sell anything.

Can I use the website instead?

The website, games.zanoski.com, stays free in a browser and sells nothing. The subscription is for the Android app.

How many games are there?

66, from 1 to 8 players. Open All games to search and filter.

Do the games use the real brand names?

No. The games have their own names, and the rules are the app's own.

Do I need an account?

Not for bots. For online play you need an account, and a verified email. A friend who opens your invite link can join as a guest.

How do I cancel?

Settings, Account, Manage or cancel in Google Play. See the runbook.

How do I get a refund?

Ask Google Play within 48 hours, then email zanoski.marko@gmail.com. See the runbook.

Do I lose my progress if I leave a game?

No. Your progress is saved. Tap Resume under Continue playing.

Is there a minimum age?

You must be 13 or older to make an account or to play online.

Which languages?

English, German, Croatian, Italian and Russian. Change it in Settings, App, Language.

Is it accessible?

There is High contrast, Colour vision assist and a Motion setting. Results use an icon and a word, never colour alone.

Does it work offline?

Bots do, after the first load. Online games need a connection. See the runbook.

What does the app store about me?

No cookies, no advertising, no trackers, nothing sold. See the privacy page and the guide.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Settings, Privacy and data: Download my data and Delete my account. Cancel the subscription first. See the guide.

How do I report someone?

Open the options for the message and tap Report this message. Or Block name. See chat and safety.

Why does it need Chrome?

Pawn Parlor is a Trusted Web Activity of the website. It runs on Chrome, so keep Chrome updated.

Does it run on my phone?

Android 10 or newer, phones and tablets.

Who makes it?

Marko Zanoski, a private person in Austria. It is a personal project, not a company.
