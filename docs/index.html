user guide

Pawn Parlor

Board and card games in one app. 66 games, no ads, no coins, no energy bars. Play against bots for free, or sit down with people online.

Play a bot in any game, take the daily challenge, watch live games, read the leaderboards and watch replays, all free. Online play against people (lobbies, invites by link or QR, ranked games and chat) needs the Pawn Parlor Online subscription, 0.99 a month, bought inside the Android app through Google Play only.

New subscribers get 7 days free, then 0.99 a month. Cancel in Google Play before the 7 days end and you are not charged. You can sign in with Google or with an email and password.

This is the user guide for version 1.1, and the place to report bugs and ask for features. The app is on Google Play as Board Card Games: Pawn Parlor.

Quick start

From install to your first game

  1. 1

    Install

    Get Pawn Parlor from Google Play. It runs on Android 10 or newer and needs Chrome.

  2. 2

    Pick a game

    Open it. You land on the home screen. Tap All games and pick a game.

  3. 3

    Play a bot

    Tap Play vs Bots and play. No account needed. To play people, tap Sign In and choose Sign in with Google or register with an email.

  4. 4

    Play people

    Open Settings, then Account, and tap Subscribe with Google Play.
