user guide

Pawn Parlor

Board and card games in one app. 66 games, no ads, no coins, no energy bars. Play against bots for free, or sit down with people online.

Play a bot in any game, take the daily challenge, watch live games, read the leaderboards and watch replays, all free. Online play against people (lobbies, invites by link or QR, ranked games and chat) needs the Pawn Parlor Online subscription, 0.99 a month, bought inside the Android app through Google Play only.

This is the user guide, and the place to report bugs and ask for features. The app is on Google Play as Board Card Games: Pawn Parlor.

  • Play every game against bots, on your phone or tablet.

online

With the subscription

  • Quick Match, lobbies and friend invites in every game.
  • Invite by link, QR code or lobby code.
  • Table chat, friends and recent opponents.
  • Ranked games and the online leaderboards.
  • Bots stay free, with or without it.
quick start

Quick start

  1. Install Pawn Parlor from Google Play. It runs on Android 10 or newer and needs Chrome.
  2. Open it. You land on the home screen. Tap All games and pick a game.
  3. Tap Play vs Bots and play. No account needed.
  4. Want people? Open Settings, then Account, and tap Subscribe with Google Play.
